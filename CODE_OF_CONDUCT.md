# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want XenonCMS to be a welcoming project for everyone. Contributors and maintainers commit to
taking part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, with respect
and without harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, including on code review.
- Keep discussions on topic and provide enough context to be useful.
- Acknowledge when you were wrong and when someone else's idea is better.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Sexualized content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing a discussion.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider unprofessional in a project space.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
official channel, as well as when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers by emailing
[xenon@grenishrai.pro](mailto:xenon@grenishrai.pro), or use the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Grenish/xenon/security) for anything sensitive.
Include what happened, where, and any links or screenshots. Reports are reviewed confidentially, and the
reporter's identity is kept private unless they agree otherwise.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide proportionate responses, from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban
from project spaces. Anyone asked to stop a behavior is expected to comply. Decisions can be appealed
by writing to the maintainers with new information.
